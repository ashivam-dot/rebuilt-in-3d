# Orbitwire recovery and opening test

First restore timely production with relevant licensed photos. The 24 October matching repair assigns one fitting
photo to each beat across the complete script, preserving existing relevance and map/opening limits.

Newly prepared Shorts carry `scene.production_version = news-shorts-2026-10-24` and `producer_sha` from `GITHUB_SHA`.
These survive publication and performance collection. Existing artifacts remain unversioned; no old upload is
retrospectively assigned to a new production cohort. Separate quakes from photo news.

Operational target: eight of ten otherwise eligible fresh candidates pass while still timely. Keep a denominator
with candidate ID, freshness, source/visual eligibility, exact failing stage, minutes and public upload ID/privacy.
Do not count stale or legitimately unillustratable stories as writer failures. Green runs alone do not pass.

After operational recovery, test this hypothesis: a concrete consequence-led first photo/line improves measured
48–54-hour engaged reach. Change only that opening; keep voice, runtime range, edit, sourcing and cadence stable.
Interleave ten mature controls and ten treatments, balancing story category, freshness, audience and slot. Inspect
the rendered opening, middle and ending, listen to the voice and verify every named person and visual before release.

Review after the last item reaches 54 hours and its lagged retention is available. Compare medians and spread,
average viewed, shares and subscribers per 1,000 engaged views. A provisional adoption rule is at least 20% higher
median engaged reach without material retention/conversion loss, followed by a repeat cohort. Missing authenticated
metrics are unknown. Ordinary public views do not substitute for engaged views; `views_24h` estimates do not
substitute for measured observations. Small, nonrandom news samples cannot establish causality.
