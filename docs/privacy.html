Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

"Rebuilt in 3D tools" is a private publishing tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Orbitwire, formerly Rebuilt in 3D (@orbitwirenews). It is not offered to the public.

What the tool accesses

With the owner's permission, through YouTube API Services, the tool uploads the channel's own videos, edits their titles, descriptions and settings, manages the channel's playlists and comments, and reads the channel's own analytics. It does not access any other user's data.

What is stored

The tool stores an OAuth sign-in credential for the owner's channel in an encrypted secret store, plus the channel's own video metadata and aggregate analytics. It collects no personal data about viewers and shares nothing with third parties.

YouTube and Google

The tool uses YouTube API Services and is bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
